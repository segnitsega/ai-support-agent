## 1. Returns and refunds

### 1.1 Return window

- Unopened products in original packaging may be returned within **7 days** of delivery for any reason.
- If the seal or box has been opened, a **10% restocking fee** applies (unless the item is defective).
- **Dead-on-arrival (DOA)** or defective devices must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery for a full refund or replacement at no fee.

### 1.2 Full refund eligibility

You are eligible for a **full refund** when:

- The device arrives defective or DOA (reported within 48 hours)
- The item does not match its listing
- You were charged more than once for the same order
- The order was not delivered within the promised delivery window and you choose to cancel

### 1.3 Non-returnable items

- Gift cards and digital downloads
- Ear tips, screen protectors, and hygiene-sensitive accessories once opened
- Items damaged by misuse, liquid damage, or unauthorized repair

### 1.4 How to start a return

1. Sign in to your Segni Electronics account  
2. Open **My Orders** → select the order → **Request return**  
3. Choose a reason and wait for a return authorization (usually within 1 business day)  
4. Pack the item securely with all accessories and the original invoice  
5. Hand the parcel to our courier when they schedule pickup (Addis Ababa) or drop it at a partner hub (other cities)

### 1.5 Refund timing

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** to the original payment method (Telebirr, CBE Birr, card, or bank transfer). Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via Telebirr or bank transfer.

## 2. Shipping and delivery

### 2.1 Delivery options

| Option | Where | Typical timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same-day express | Addis Ababa (selected areas) | Order before 12:00 → delivery same day | Extra fee applies |
| Standard city | Addis Ababa | 1–2 business days | Free above ETB 2,500 |
| Nationwide | Major Ethiopian cities | 3–5 business days | Timing varies by destination |
| Remote areas | Smaller towns | 5–8 business days | May require pickup from a local hub |

### 2.2 Shipping fees

- Free standard shipping inside Addis Ababa for orders **ETB 2,500+**
- Same-day express: from **ETB 150** (zone-dependent)
- Nationwide: calculated at checkout based on weight and city

### 2.3 Tracking

After dispatch you receive an SMS/email with a tracking link. You can also open **My Orders** in your account. For live status of a specific order number, ask support with your order ID (for example `#1234`) so an agent can look it up.

### 2.4 Changing a delivery address

You may update the shipping address **only before the order is dispatched**.

1. Go to **My Orders** → select the order → **Edit shipping address**  
2. If status is **Out for delivery**, address changes are not available online — contact support immediately with your order number. Same-day Addis redirects are not guaranteed.

### 2.5 Failed delivery

If nobody is available to receive the parcel:

- The courier will retry once the next business day  
- After two failed attempts, the order is held for **5 days** at the local hub  
- Uncollected orders are returned to our warehouse and refunded minus outbound shipping (if applicable)

### 3.1 Accepted methods

- **Telebirr**
- **CBE Birr**
- **Bank transfer** (commercial banks in Ethiopia)
- **Cash on delivery (COD)** — Addis Ababa only

Business accounts placing bulk orders may request **Net-30 invoicing** via sales@segnielectronics.com.

### 3.2 When you are charged

- Digital wallet and card payments are authorized at checkout  
- Bank transfer orders are confirmed after payment is verified (usually within 1 business day)  
- COD is collected on delivery; please prepare exact change when possible

### 3.3 Invoices

- Open **My Orders** → **View invoice** for any confirmed order  
- Invoices are also emailed within **24 hours** of shipment  
- Business accounts can download a monthly consolidated invoice under **Account → Billing**  
- Missing invoice? Email support@segnielectronics.com with your order number

## 4. Orders (before shipping)

### 4.1 Placing an order

1. Add items to cart and proceed to checkout  
2. Confirm shipping address and payment method  
3. You will receive an order confirmation by email/SMS with your order number

### 4.2 Editing or removing items

You may modify or remove items **free of charge** within **2 hours** of placing the order, or any time before the order status becomes **Processing** — whichever comes first.

1. **My Orders** → select the order → **Edit order**  
2. Once the order has **shipped**, it cannot be changed. Receive the item and use the return process instead.

### 4.3 Cancelling an order

- **Before shipping:** cancel free of charge for a full refund (3–5 business days)  
- **After dispatch:** a **5% handling fee** may apply to cover return logistics  
- **Pre-orders:** can be cancelled free of charge any time before the device ships

Cancel from **My Orders → Cancel order**, or contact support with your order number if the button is unavailable.

## 5. Warranty and defective products

### 5.1 Standard warranty

Most new devices sold by Segni Electronics include a **12-month limited warranty** against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date. Accessories (chargers, cables sold separately) typically include **6 months**.

Warranty covers:

- Manufacturing defects under normal use  
- Hardware failures not caused by the customer

Warranty does **not** cover:

- Accidental damage, drops, liquid damage  
- Unauthorized repairs or modified software  
- Normal wear (battery capacity decline within expected range, cosmetic scratches)

### 5.2 How to claim warranty

1. Contact support with your order number, device serial/IMEI, and a short description  
2. Our team may ask for photos or a troubleshooting checklist  
3. If approved, we arrange pickup/repair or replacement according to stock and warranty terms

## 6. Account and security

### 6.1 Creating an account

Register with your email or phone number at segni-electronics.com/register. Verify via the code sent to you, then complete your profile and default shipping address.

### 6.2 Password reset

1. Open the login page → **Forgot password**  
2. Enter your email or phone number  
3. Use the one-time code (valid for **10 minutes**) to set a new password  
4. If you do not receive a code, check spam, wait 2 minutes, and try again. Still stuck? Contact support — do not share OTPs with anyone claiming to be support.

### 6.3 Editing account details

Update name, phone, email, and saved addresses under **Account → Profile**. Email changes require re-verification.

### 6.4 Deleting an account

You may request account deletion under **Account → Privacy → Delete account**.  
Open orders must be completed or cancelled first. Deletion removes personal profile data according to our privacy policy; invoices needed for tax/warranty may be retained as required by law.

### 7.2 What to include

Always share your **order number**, contact phone, and a short description of the issue. That helps us look up status or open a ticket faster.

### 7.3 When we open a support ticket

If your issue cannot be solved from this handbook (for example a device that will not power on after troubleshooting), ask us to **open a support ticket**. A specialist reviews the draft before it is filed in our system.

### 7.4 Escalation

If you need a human specialist immediately — for example unresolved complaints, account-closure disputes, or legal matters — ask to speak with a manager or human agent. Those requests are routed to our escalation team.
